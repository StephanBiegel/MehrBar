<interfaces>
Summary CSV row (one per run, appended to `--out`; header written once): label,variant,count,mode,transparent,size,seconds,frames,cost_p50,cost_p95,cost_p99,cadence_p50,cadence_p95,cadence_p99,drop_pct,cpu_total_pct,core_eq,dwm_cpu_total_pct,dwm_frames_dropped,dwm_frames_missed,gc0,gc1,gc2,alloc_per_frame,idle_frames,idle_cpu_pct,effective_mode,tier,pm_rows.
Definitions: cost = Rendering handler + OnRender time (ms); cadence = delta of RenderingEventArgs.RenderingTime (ms) with duplicates skipped; dropped frame = cadence > 25 ms (1.5 periods at 60 Hz); cpu_total_pct = ΔCPU / (wall × Environment.ProcessorCount) × 100 (the Task-Manager column, D-19); core_eq = ΔCPU / wall; dwm values `n/a` when dwm.exe cannot be opened at normal integrity.
CLI: `Driver render --variant R1|R2 --count 20|64 --render hw|sw [--transparent true|false] [--size WxH] [--seconds 30] [--warmup 5] [--idle 5] [--synthetic] [--manual --seconds N] [--label L] [--out csv] [--frames-dir dir]`.
Matrix output: `spikes/results/q4-<label>-summary.csv` (committed) and raw per-frame CSVs under `RepoPaths.PrivateResults/q4-raw/<label>/` (ignored; large).
</interfaces>
